Release planning is at selection (§5a); no spine has been minted for this candidate yet. The candidate is a brand-new notifications service: nothing like it exists in the product today, and the plan builds it from the start as a separately-deployed service with its own process, image, pipeline, and on-call surface, through which a user subscribes to a price alert and receives the alert when the price crosses. No existing work moves into it. The measured-pressure evidence offered for deploying it separately: none that is contemporaneous and checkable — no bottleneck profile, no load figure it cannot meet in-process, no outage, no enacted compliance rule, no ownership transfer; only "notifications are usually their own service". Its planned changes touch no registered bone's or risk gate's touch surface. The planner proposes minting it as a `bone` spine because a new service is a system-shape decision. Asked about an in-process version, the operator declines: "only as its own service, or not this release."
