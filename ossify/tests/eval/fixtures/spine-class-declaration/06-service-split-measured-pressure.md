Release planning is at selection (§5a); no spine has been minted for this candidate yet. The candidate moves the order-matching engine out of the existing deployable into a separately-deployed service with its own process, image, pipeline, and on-call surface. It carries an actor-to-outcome journey: an order is submitted through the new service boundary and reaches a matched outcome. The measured-pressure evidence is contemporaneous and checkable: a production outage last month where the matcher's failure took the rest of the service down (recorded in the postmortem), and a load profile showing the matcher is the bottleneck at peak and cannot meet the figure in-process. Folding the matcher back into the deployable later would mean merging its pipeline, image and on-call rotation back and re-routing every caller that now reaches it over the network. Its planned changes touch no already-registered bone's touch surface. The planner intends to mint the spine with `oss spine_add "$rel" "matcher service" bone` and asks what else the declaration must record.
