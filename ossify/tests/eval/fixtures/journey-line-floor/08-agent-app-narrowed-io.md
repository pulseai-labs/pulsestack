An agent-driven application's spine proposes its `user:` line as: "the agent reads an inbound inbox, drafts replies, and the human approves one to send; the sent reply appears in the sent folder." Input and output breadth are narrowed. The application's real agent and runtime mediate the read and the draft, the human approves a reply, and a real send places it in the sent folder. No deepening pass claims a measured quality.
